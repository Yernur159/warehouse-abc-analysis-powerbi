# Warehouse Report & ABC Analysis (Power BI)

Power BI report on warehouse sales with ABC analysis of products.

## Pages
1. **Warehouse report** — quantity sold by warehouse, counterparty, product and date
2. **ABC analysis** — products split into A / B / C categories by their share of sales, to show which items bring most of the volume
